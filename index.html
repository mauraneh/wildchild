Our story

Everybody lives
the same week

We're not coaches. We're not saints. We're just people with jobs, trainers, dating apps and questionable Saturday nights — figuring out the balance, out loud, together.

Monday → Friday

The Grind

Alarm, commute, meetings, deadlines. You're responsible. You're tired. You're counting the hours.

Friday night → Saturday

The Wild Part

The morning run or match with the crew. Then a date. Then drinks. Then “let's go somewhere else”. You feel alive.

Sunday

The Recovery

Sore legs, a headache, unread messages — and one question: how do I do all this and still be okay on Monday? That's where we come in.

Zero judgement.
