# WILD CHILD · The Sunday Recovery Show

> **Gym at 6 am. Drinks at 7 pm. Regrets by noon.**
> FR : *Salle à 6h. Apéro à 19h. Regrets à midi.*

## The name

**WILD CHILD**, because none of us really grew up. Monday to Friday we're
adults with badges, inboxes and performance reviews. Friday night to Saturday
night we're the wild child again: the "quick" run, the five-a-side, the first
date, the "okay, one last drink". And on Sunday we pay for it.

Full title: **WILD CHILD · The Sunday Recovery Show**
(FR : *Le podcast de la récup du dimanche*). New episode every Sunday, late
morning. Let's be realistic.

## The story

Everybody lives the same week.

**Monday → Friday: the grind.** Alarm, commute, meetings that could have been
an e-mail. You play the responsible adult and count the hours.

**The house rule.** Gym at 6 am, drinks at 7 pm. We destroy ourselves at the
gym at dawn so we've "earned" the drinks that night. Flawless logic.

**Friday night → Saturday: the slip-up.** A brutal Saturday session to
"compensate". Then a date. Then a well-deserved drink. Then "one last one".
You know how this ends.

**Sunday: the recovery.** Sore legs, pounding head, 14 unread messages and a
vague sense of shame. And one question: how do we survive Monday?

**WILD CHILD** is the friend who shows up on Sunday with coffee and zero
judgement (okay, a little). Every week the hosts debrief their own weekend:
the goals, the fails, the ghostings and the regrets. We're not coaches, not
therapists, and definitely not role models. We're looking for balance out
loud. Spoiler: we haven't found it.

### Tone of voice

Think *Sex and the City* recorded at Sunday brunch: a narrator voice that
wonders out loud ("and we couldn't help but wonder…"), two characters with
clear roles, and total honesty about dating, drinking and bad decisions.


- **Sarcastic, never mean.** We laugh at ourselves first.
- **Natural.** We talk like we talk to friends, not like a wellness brand.
- **Honest.** Real stories, our own disasters first.
- **No lectures.** Party or not, sober or not, single or not.
- **Still a bit useful.** Every episode ends with one tip that's actually doable.
- **Writing rule:** no long dashes (—) in site copy. Short sentences, full stops.

## Listener questions

Listeners send questions from the website ("Pose ta question"). Every Sunday
the hosts pick a few and answer them on air, anonymously if asked, with advice
they don't follow themselves.

## Episode format (about 45 min)
