# WILD CHILD — The Sunday Recovery Show

> **Work hard. Play harder. Recover smarter.**

## The name

**WILD CHILD** — because none of us really grew up. From Monday to Friday we're
adults with badges, inboxes and performance reviews. From Friday night to
Saturday night we're the wild child again: the 10k run, the five-a-side, the
first date, the “one last drink”. And on Sunday… we have to face the damage.

The full show title is **WILD CHILD — The Sunday Recovery Show**, released every
Sunday morning, right when you need it.

## The story

Everybody lives the same week.

**Monday → Friday — The Grind.** Alarm, commute, meetings, deadlines. You're
responsible. You're tired. You're counting the hours.

**Friday night → Saturday — The Wild Part.** You finally breathe. Morning run
or match with the crew, then a date, then drinks, then “let's go somewhere
else”. You feel alive.

**Sunday — The Recovery.** Legs sore, phone full of messages you don't want to
read, a headache, and that little voice: *“How do I do all of this and still
be okay on Monday?”*

**WILD CHILD** is the friend who shows up on Sunday with coffee and zero
judgement. Every week, the hosts debrief their own weekend — the wins, the
fails, the ghostings, the PRs and the regrets — and turn it into honest,
funny, useful talk about living fully **without burning out**.

We're not coaches. We're not saints. We're just figuring out the balance, out
loud, together.

### Mission

Help people who work hard all week enjoy their weekends to the max — sport,
love life, nights out — and still feel good in their body, their head and
their bank account on Monday.

### Tone of voice

- **Honest** — we share real stories, our own fails first.
- **Funny** — it's Sunday, nobody wants a lecture.
- **No judgement** — party or no party, sober or not, single or taken.
- **Useful** — every episode ends with something you can apply this week.

## Episode format (≈ 45 min)

| Segment | What happens |
|---|---|
| **01 · The Scoreboard** | Sport recap of the weekend: the run, the match, the gym, the PRs and the injuries. |
| **02 · The Swipe Report** | Dating stories: the matches, the dates, the red flags, the listener DMs. |
| **03 · The Damage Report** | The night out and the morning after: hangover rating /10, what worked, what didn't. |
| **04 · The Monday Reset** | The balance part: sleep, food, recovery, money, mindset. One actionable tip for the week. |
